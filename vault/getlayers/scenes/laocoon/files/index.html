Metal
in Motion

Metal was never meant to sit still. We pour it, light it, and let it run — a surface that shifts second by second, live in your browser.

MOLT3N is the digital sculpture studio built by the PUDDL3 team. One belief carried over: a thing made to move should never wait.

Second
